How to Install Your .mcaddon
1
Click ⚔️ FORGE WEAPON and download the .mcaddon file.
2
On your device, open the Files app and find the downloaded file. Tap it — Minecraft opens automatically and imports both packs!
3
On PC: double-click the .mcaddon file. Minecraft Bedrock (Windows 10/11 edition) will open and import it automatically.
4
In Minecraft, go to Play → Create New World (or edit existing) → Add-Ons → add both the Resource Pack and Behavior Pack from "My Packs".
5
Start the world, then type in chat: /give @s weaponforge:<weapon_id> — your custom weapon appears in your hand!
💡
The item ID is shown in the preview panel. Replace spaces with underscores and use lowercase — e.g. weaponforge:shadow_fang
