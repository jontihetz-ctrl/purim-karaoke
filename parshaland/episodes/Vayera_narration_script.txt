PARSHALAND — Parshas Vayera — narration script

Recording tips
- Quiet, soft room (a bedroom with curtains/duvet is perfect). Phone a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side. Airplane mode on.
- Read it all in one go, with about 2 seconds of silence between lines.
- Made a mistake? Just pause, and read the whole line again from the start. I keep the last try.
- This week: a little SLOWER than last time, warm, like a bedtime story.

1. This week's parsha is Parshas Vayera!

2. Three days after his bris milah, Avraham was sitting at the door of his tent, by the trees of Mamre.

3. Hashem came to visit him, to teach us the mitzvah of visiting the sick.

4. It was a very hot day. Hashem made it hot so that no travelers would bother Avraham while he was healing. But Avraham was sad, because he loved having guests!

5. Then Avraham looked up, and saw three men standing near him. They were really malachim, but they looked like travelers.

6. Avraham ran to greet them, even though he was in pain! Please, come rest under the tree, and let me bring water to wash your feet.

7. He hurried to Sarah: quick, bake fresh bread! Then he ran to prepare the best meat, with butter and milk, and served his guests himself.

8. Where is Sarah your wife? asked the guests. In the tent, said Avraham.

9. This time next year, Sarah will have a son!

10. Sarah laughed to herself. I'm ninety years old, how could I have a baby? But Hashem said: is anything too hard for Hashem?

11. Hashem told Avraham that the cities of Sedom and Amorah were very wicked, and would be destroyed.

12. Avraham begged Hashem: what if there are fifty tzaddikim there? Will You save the city for them? And Hashem said yes.

13. What about forty-five? Forty? Thirty? Twenty? Ten? Each time Hashem said yes. But there were not even ten.

14. Two of the malachim came to Sedom, where Lot lived. Lot had learned from Avraham to welcome guests, so he invited them in.

15. In the morning the malachim said: hurry, take your family and run! And don't look back!

16. Hashem rained fire and sulfur on Sedom and Amorah. Lot's wife looked back, and she became a pillar of salt.

17. And then, just as Hashem promised, Sarah had a baby boy! Avraham was one hundred years old, and Sarah was ninety.

18. They named him Yitzchak, which means he will laugh, because everyone who heard the news laughed with joy.

19. When Yitzchak was eight days old, Avraham gave him a bris milah. And when he grew bigger, Avraham made a great feast.

20. When Yitzchak was grown up, Hashem gave Avraham his biggest test of all.

21. Take your son, your only one, whom you love, Yitzchak, and go to Har HaMoriah, and bring him up there as a korban.

22. Avraham got up early in the morning, happy to do what Hashem wanted. After three days, they saw a cloud resting on the mountain.

23. Where is the sheep for the korban? asked Yitzchak. Hashem will show us, said Avraham. And the two of them walked on together.

24. Avraham built a mizbe'ach and placed Yitzchak on it. Yitzchak was ready too.

25. Suddenly, a malach of Hashem called out: Avraham! Avraham! Do not harm the boy! Now I know that you fear Hashem.

26. Avraham looked up and saw a ram, caught by its horns in a bush. He brought the ram as a korban instead of Yitzchak.

27. Hashem said: because you did this, I will bless you, and make your children as many as the stars in the sky and the sand on the seashore.

28. And through your children, the whole world will be blessed.

29. What happens next? That's a story for next week!

30. Good Shabbos!
